Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Site Data Crawler's architecture and capabilities.

Does Site Data Crawler send any crawled data to external servers?
No. Site Data Crawler is 100% client-side. All HTTP requests, sitemap parsing, and HTML text extractions happen strictly inside your browser environment. No tracking, no external API relays, and zero telemetry.
How does the crawler respect robots.txt and rate limits?
When starting a crawl, the engine fetches and parses /robots.txt. It respects Disallow directives, extracts Sitemap paths, and adheres to configurable polite delays (default 500ms between requests) with controlled worker concurrency (default 3 simultaneous workers).
What are Scope & Extra Pages rules?
Scope & Extra Pages rules automatically detect and prioritize critical compliance and utility pages (Terms of Service, Privacy Policies, Cookie Notices, Disclaimers, Imprint, and Contact Us) at the front of the crawl queue, ensuring they are cataloged even if maximum crawl limits are hit.
Can I crawl Single Page Applications (SPAs) and dynamic websites?
Yes! In addition to multi-page background crawling, the extension provides an "Active Tab Rendered DOM Extractor" tool that inspects the live rendered JavaScript DOM of any currently open tab.
What export formats are supported?
You can export in 6 different formats: Heading-Structured Markdown (.md), Heading-Structured Text (.txt), Standard Spreadsheet CSV (.csv), Structured JSON (.json), XML Sitemap (.xml), and Complete ASCII SEO Audit Report (.txt).
